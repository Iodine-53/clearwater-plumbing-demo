Polybutylene is a gray plastic supply pipe installed in most Florida homes built between 1978 and 1995 — and insurers here treat it as a ticking time bomb. Look under sinks or at your water heater connections for gray flexible pipe with plastic or copper crimp fittings. We can confirm it in one visit and lay out a PEX repipe plan, which most carriers require before they'll write or renew a policy.

Watch for a water bill that spikes for no reason, warm or damp spots on the floor, the sound of running water when everything's off, or cracks appearing in tile. Florida's slab foundations hide leaks until they've done real damage — our acoustic and infrared detection finds the exact spot so we fix the pipe, not your whole floor.
